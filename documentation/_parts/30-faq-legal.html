14 Troubleshooting

Common problems and how to fix them.

  1. Check Apache is running in WAMP/XAMPP.
  2. Check the URL matches your project folder name (e.g. http://localhost/ERP_qubeteck/).
  3. Enable mod_rewrite in your Apache config; the system uses the bundled .htaccess.
  4. Check the MySQL database "stock" exists and was imported.

Check that you entered the exact TT number, that the payment was actually completed, and that your server has Internet access to transactioninfo.ethiotelecom.et. If the customer paid before midnight, it will not pass the same-day check today.

16 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Who we are

This documentation and the SOORA ERP system are provided by the business running the system (your company). This policy explains how the system handles information.

2. Information the system stores

  • Account information — usernames, email addresses, names, phone numbers and password hashes of staff users.
  • Business data — products, orders, customers, payments and reports entered while using the system.
  • Customer contact details — names, addresses and phone numbers of individuals and organizations you sell to.
  • SMS data — for numbers enabled for SMS, message logs (order, status and payment notifications) retained for delivery tracking.

3. How information is protected

  • Passwords are stored with strong, one-way hashing (bcrypt). No plain-text passwords are kept, and nobody, including administrators, can read them.
  • Access to each area is controlled by permissions set per user group.
  • The system runs on your own server, so your data stays on your infrastructure and under your control.

4. Data sharing

The system shares only what is necessary to provide its functions: SMS messages are sent through the GeeSMS gateway; Telebirr verification queries Ethio Telecom's receipt service with the transaction number you provide. Data is not sold to third parties.

5. Data retention and your rights

Data is retained as long as your business needs it. As a business owner you may export or delete business records at any time. Employees and customers should contact the business operating the system to request access, correction or deletion of their personal data.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the system grows. The latest version is always published here with a revision date.
